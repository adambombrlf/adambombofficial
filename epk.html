Loud. Live. Still dangerous.

July 2026: three arenas with Scorpions in Lyon, Paris and Strasbourg, nearly 11,000 people in Strasbourg alone. Then straight back to the clubs to headline. Arena-tested, club-hungry, and booking 2027 now.

  • The show A power trio, screaming guitars, smoke, sparks, flares and real fire: a burning guitar, burning cymbals and a fire-breathing bassist. 40 minutes as support, up to two hours as headliner.
  • The pedigree Geffen Records, Leber-Krebs management (AC/DC, Aerosmith), records cut with members of AC/DC and Montrose.
  • The new stuff LiveDream, produced by Scorpions producer Dieter Dierks, now on vinyl and digipack via Necktwister.

On the road

  • Three arenas with Scorpions: LDLC Arena Lyon (14 July) · Accor Arena Paris (17 July) · Zénith Strasbourg (20 July, ~11,000 people).
  • 20+ headline club shows in France, Belgium, Germany, Italy, Austria, Czechia and the Baltics around them.
  • Special guest of Extreme in Germany: Live Music Hall Cologne and Garage Saarbrücken (August 2025).
  • 1,700+ shows in 26 countries since 2003 · tour archive.

Who he is

  • Geffen Records debut with Cliff Williams (AC/DC) on bass.
  • Guitar lesson from Eddie Van Halen; on stage with Chuck Berry and John Paul Jones (Led Zeppelin).
  • Toured with Metallica, W.A.S.P., Dio, Yngwie Malmsteen and Michael Monroe; special guest of Extreme (2025) and Scorpions (2026).
  • Bang Your Head, Bloodstock, Metal Days. Up to 200 shows a year.

Band: Adam Bomb (vocals, guitar) · Paul Del Bello (bass) · Kid Leo (drums).
