No cookies, no tracking

This website itself does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising pixels or other tracking tools.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit the site, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GitHub privacy statement.

Spotify player

The Spotify player on the homepage is only loaded after you click “Load Spotify player”. From that moment Spotify receives data such as your IP address and may set cookies. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Spotify’s privacy policy applies. You withdraw your consent simply by reloading the page; the player will not load again unless you click.

When you email us

If you contact us by email (booking or label/management), we use your name, email address and the content of your message only to answer you and to prepare or carry out a booking. Legal basis: steps prior to entering into a contract and performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), or our legitimate interest in answering your question (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep these emails as long as needed for that purpose and for as long as accounting and tax rules require. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Links to other sites

Links to Facebook, YouTube, Instagram and Bandcamp take you to those platforms; their own privacy rules then apply. No data are sent to them just by visiting this site.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of processing, data portability, and you can object to processing (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Send your request to info@necktwister.com; we answer within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit), Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be (Art. 77 GDPR).
